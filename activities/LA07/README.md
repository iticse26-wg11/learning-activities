# Inductive LLM Explainability Pipeline

**Related ILOs:** [MM08](https://github.com/iticse26-wg11/intended-learning-outcomes/blob/main/ilos/MM08.md), [EPR06](https://github.com/iticse26-wg11/intended-learning-outcomes/blob/main/ilos/EPR06.md)

**Type:** In-class, small groups, digital · **Duration:** 1 hour · **Scale:** Small and large cohorts

## Description

This activity consists of two tasks: research and reflection.

*Task 1 - Research* The instructor provides students with 10 outputs from a GenAI system to fact check. Output generated by the instructor should ideally contain some outputs that are intentionally false or in some cases somewhat difficult to factcheck. Possible themes the instructor can pick from include - information from a trusted source such as a government website, news article, prominent person and location check, soccer player/club. The students are tasked to work in pairs to factcheck the provided outputs. Once initial fact checking is done, still working in pairs, students attempt to develop and manipulate prompts to search for alternative sources to further verify their results.

*Task 2 - Reflection* The instructor provides students with definitions of reliable, trustworthy, and responsible GenAI systems. Using the fact checking example above, they assess the reliable characteristics of the output they received. The session closes with the instructor leading a discussion on the difference between reliable, trustworthy, and responsible GenAI systems.

## Keywords

Fact-checking, source verification, reliability, trustworthiness, responsible AI

## Prerequisites

- Explanation of reliable and responsible systems

## Resources

- Computers, pre-generated AI system output
