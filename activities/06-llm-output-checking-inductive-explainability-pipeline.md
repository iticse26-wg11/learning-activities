# LLM Output-Checking: Inductive Explainability Pipeline

**Related ILOs:** MM08, EPR06
**Type:** In-class, Small groups, Digital · **Duration:** 60 minutes · **Scale:** large and small cohorts

## Description

**Task 1 — Research task.** The instructor provides students with 10 outputs from a GenAI system to fact-check. Output generated by the instructor should ideally contain some outputs that are intentionally false or in some cases somewhat difficult to fact-check. Possible themes the instructor can pick from include: information from a trusted source such as a government website, news article, prominent person and location check, soccer player/club.

The students are tasked to work in pairs to fact-check the provided outputs. Once initial fact-checking is done, still working in pairs, students attempt to develop and manipulate prompts to search for alternative sources to further verify their results.

**Task 2 — Reflection.** The instructor provides students with definitions of reliable, trustworthy, and responsible GenAI systems. Using the fact-checking example above, they assess the reliability characteristics of the output they received.

The session closes with the instructor leading a discussion on the difference between reliable, trustworthy, and responsible GenAI systems. The instructor also provides examples of (or links to) such systems/frameworks/models.
