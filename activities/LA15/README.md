# AI Application Project

**Related ILOs** (at the end of the course, students should be able to …)
- [EPR11](https://github.com/iticse26-wg11/intended-learning-outcomes/blob/main/ilos/EPR11.md): design a plan for the responsible and context-appropriate use of GenAI, including documenting of GenAI-related outputs and decisions
- [EPR13](https://github.com/iticse26-wg11/intended-learning-outcomes/blob/main/ilos/EPR13.md): discuss societal perceptions of and expectations around GenAI use, taking into consideration power dynamics in the adoption of GenAI tools.
- [EPR14](https://github.com/iticse26-wg11/intended-learning-outcomes/blob/main/ilos/EPR14.md): discuss frameworks for the fair and responsible use of GenAI in the student's particular discipline.
- [CS07](https://github.com/iticse26-wg11/intended-learning-outcomes/blob/main/ilos/CS07.md): apply prompt refinement techniques and evaluate outputs iteratively in order to improve alignment between generated content and intended outcomes.

**Type:** In-class, individual or small group, digital · **Duration:** 2 hours · **Scale:** Small and large cohorts

## Description

This activity consists of four parts. Students are tasked with using GenAI for planning a task, they use interactive prompting to perform the task, reflect individually then discuss in groups. This activity aims to enable students to produce an artifact, critically reflect on the creation of the artifact while considering fair and responsible use of GenAI systems. The instructor provides students with a specific subtopic, e.g., the relational model or password security. Students are tasked with using GenAI to come up with a presentation on the topic assigned.

**Task 1: Individual Planning.** Students are tasked to come up with a plan on how they intend to delegate the task to the GenAI system e.g., creation of the presentation, list of possible sources.

**Task 2: Individual Prompting.** Students select a GenAI system of choice to complete this task iteratively until they are fine with the outcome. Students also submit a log of prompt logs and a reflection of their intentions behind each prompt.

**Task 3: Individual Reflection.** After completing the prompting task, students are asked to reflect through questions such as:

- Do you think that you learned the subtopic through this process?
- How would you describe the quality of the outcome provided initially and after prompt iteration?
- How would you rate the level of contribution of the final outcome between you and the GenAI system (e.g., 40% human, 60% GenAI)?
- Did you stick with your original plan? What changes did you make?

**Task 4: Discussion.** In class, students work in groups of 4-5 to discuss their experience considering societal perception and expectations around GenAI in producing educational material. Next, they review the OECD AI Principles, select two of them at their choice and discuss how they relate to their discipline
