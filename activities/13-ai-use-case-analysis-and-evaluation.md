# AI Use Case Analysis and Evaluation

**Related ILOs:** MM04, MM06, CS04a, CS04b
**Type:** In-class, Individual and Small groups, Digital · **Duration:** 20 minutes per use case + 30 minutes for the final discussion · **Scale:** large and small cohorts

## Description

Based on the number of students and the allocated time, instructors assign students a number of different use cases related to GenAI, ranging from 2 to 4 (e.g. summarize a text, produce an activity report, prepare a presentation, write code, …). Students work individually on each of them for a fixed time, reflect on the outcomes and document their observations, considering:

- the correctness and the quality of the output
- the presence of factual errors, omissions, unsupported assumptions, biased framing, security flaws, and/or potentially harmful consequences
- the added value of using an LLM for this task
- the appropriateness of using an LLM for this task based on ethical considerations

Instructors can provide a rubric to help students evaluate the different aspects of the outcome.

Instructors may decide to divide students into two or more groups that have different use cases and/or a different order of tasks. Instructors can also decide to have students use different LLMs, to compare outputs in the final discussion.

Students can proceed to the next use case only after completing the reflection task on the previous one. The observations of the students can be collected through a paper worksheet or an online form.

At the end, the observations are discussed as a full-class activity, and the instructor can propose discussion points such as "For which tasks is AI more useful? For which tasks are ethical considerations of mandatory importance? Where do you see the best performance?".
