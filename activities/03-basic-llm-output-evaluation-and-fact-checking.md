# Basic LLM Output Evaluation and Fact Checking

**Related ILOs:** EPR04
**Type:** In-lab, Individual, Digital · **Duration:** 60 minutes · **Scale:** large and small cohorts

## Description

The students are provided with a scenario (e.g. creating a plan for a class party) and are tasked with prompting at least two different GenAI systems of choice to achieve this task. Students are encouraged to compare the output from each GenAI system and identify the differences, shortcomings and limitations of each tool. Each student then selects the most robust-looking output (e.g. alignment with prompt, extent of detail) and evaluates it against a provided policy (e.g. a short list of constraints or ethical rules that the output must not cross).

The session closes with the instructor leading a discussion around the shortcomings of GenAI outputs and the need to follow policies.

## Keywords

Model comparison, Fact-checking, Policy compliance, Output quality, Limitations

None

- Computer systems per student
