# Anatomy of (Another) AI System

**Related ILOs:** [EPR01](https://github.com/iticse26-wg11/intended-learning-outcomes/blob/main/ilos/EPR01.md), [EPR09](https://github.com/iticse26-wg11/intended-learning-outcomes/blob/main/ilos/EPR09.md)
**Type:** In-class, Small groups, Offline · **Duration:** 1 hour · **Scale:** 1 or more small groups

## Description

Inspired by Crawford and Joler's 2018 "Anatomy of an AI System", student groups are tasked with researching, tracing, and relating (e.g. diagramming) the material, labor, and data resources involved in the training and deploying of a chosen (or assigned) real-world AI system (e.g. ChatGPT, self-driving car). Students leverage publicly available sources and reports to investigate the resource implications of the AI system, with the goal of quantifying or estimating the demand on key resources (e.g. total electricity cost of model training). When finished, students briefly summarize key findings, seeding a full-class discussion on the aggregate effects of growing demand for AI systems, especially in computing and software development domains.

## Keywords

Resource consumption, Labour, Data, Environmental impact, AI infrastructure

## Prerequisites

Discussion of types of resources (raw materials, electricity and water, human labor, data) involved in AI systems and the costs (i.e. impacts) of their extraction.

- Poster paper

## Assessment

Formative assessment evaluating depth and specificity of the group's research, and the group's ability to connect findings to each other and to earlier class discussions/readings.
